# Let veteran state employees buy their military retirement credit at a fair price

**Joint Leadership Council of Veterans Service Organizations — 2027 Session**
One sentence, in one Code section: **§ 51.1-142.2**. **General fund cost: $0.**

---

## OBJECTIVE

Amend the Virginia Retirement System so a veteran who works for Virginia can buy up to
four years of military service credit at the regular price **at any point in their
career** — not only in their first two years on the job.

---

## DISCUSSION

Veterans on the state payroll may **already** buy up to four years of their military
service toward their VRS pension. The price is a flat percentage of salary, set by the
VRS Board.

**But that price expires 24 months after their first day of work.** After that, the same
four years costs far more.

Three reasons that deadline is the wrong deadline:

- **It hits at the worst possible time.** Four years of credit costs about **$18,666**.
  The average new state hire is **37.7 years old** — just out of uniform, often taking a
  pay cut, and at the bottom of the state pay scale.
- **They cannot pay it off slowly.** VRS payment plans run 6 to 12 months and cannot
  extend past the two-year deadline. Pay fast, or lose the price for good.
- **Most never hear about it in time.** It is one line in a long statute, explained on
  day one with the rest of the new-hire paperwork. The average state employee stays
  **10.3 years** — five times longer than the window.

**The result:** a veteran with eight years of state service pays dramatically more for the
same four years of military credit than the brand-new hire down the hall. Same service to
the country, same credit, different price — and the only difference is how long they have
worked for Virginia.

**Who it helps:** about **3,400** veterans now working for the Commonwealth, plus roughly
**600** more hired every year.

---

## COST

**Under $2 million one time. Under $100,000 a year after that. No general fund money.**

That one-time figure is about **5 cents out of every $100** of a single year's classified
state payroll.

It is small because most veterans already pay close to full value. Everyone hired since
2014 is in the Hybrid plan, where a year of credit is worth only 1% of pay — so the
regular price nearly covers the real cost already.

> These are JLCVSO analyst estimates built from published VRS rates — **not VRS figures.**
> VRS must file an official impact statement on any VRS bill (§ 30-19.1:7). JLCVSO should
> ask VRS for its number **before** the bill is filed.

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**Amend and reenact § 51.1-142.2 of the Code of Virginia to:**

1. **Drop the two-year price deadline for military service only.** Leave it in place for
   every other kind of purchased service, so the bill stays narrow.
2. **Let veterans pay over up to 60 months** by payroll deduction.
3. **Give the VRS Board two guardrails:** authority to charge a somewhat higher published
   rate at older ages, and authority to require the purchase be finished 12 months before
   retirement. These stop last-minute buying.

No regulation change is needed — the price rule is in the statute, so this takes a bill.
