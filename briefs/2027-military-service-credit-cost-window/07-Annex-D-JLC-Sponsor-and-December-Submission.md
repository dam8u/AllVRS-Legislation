## 4. Why the Winter Council is the gate, and not the June convention

This is the finding that the decision changes most.

The JLC screen sits in **late April**. The VFW Department Convention sits in **June**.
So if the Department's position had to come from a convention resolution, **the sponsor
would arrive at the screen with nothing adopted** — and the resolution would land in
the same week as the full-Council presentation, three days before the indicative paper
deadline. That ordering is wrong and it is fixable, because the Department has **two
instruments, not one**, and the faster one is documented.

| Instrument | Who acts | Documented authority | Timing fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Legislative Committee's prioritized listing** | Legislative Committee (min. 7 members) → Department Commander and Council of Administration | SOP: the committee *"shall recommend in writing to the Department Commander and Council of Administration a prioritized listing of state and local legislative measures it feels worthy of support by the membership."* | **Fits.** A written recommendation needs no convention. The COA "shall govern the affairs of this Department between annual Department Conventions" and meets "not less than twice each year," with the Commander able to call a special meeting. **Winter Council 2027-01-13 is the scheduled venue.** |
| **Convention resolution** | Member → Post approval → Post Adjutant → Department Adjutant → Resolutions Committee → Department Convention floor | SOP § 3, verbatim chain. *"All resolutions of statewide, regional, or national concern must be acted upon by the Department Convention."* | **Does not fit the screen, fits everything after it.** June 2027. This is the durable instrument — and the one that makes the position survive a change of Department officers. |

**So the sequence that works, if JLCVSO chooses to make the approach:** the prioritized
listing carries the initiative into the **April screen** and the **June full-Council
presentation**; the **convention resolution ratifies it** during the same June window
and becomes the standing Department position for the 2028 session and after. Those are
complementary, not alternatives.

**Confidence.** **High** that both instruments exist and that the authority language
is as quoted — both are from the Department's own approved SOP. **Medium** on the
sequencing recommendation itself, which is my inference from the two calendars, not a
documented VFW practice. **Not documented at all:** whether the Department would use
the listing route for an item outside its current priorities. That is U-12.

**One timing trap inside the resolution route.** A resolution originates with a member
and **must be approved by the author's Post** before the Post Adjutant forwards it to
the Department Adjutant. Post-level action is monthly at best. Working backwards from a
June 2027 convention, the originating Post step belongs in **winter or early spring
2027**, not May. Confidence **High** on the chain (SOP § 3 verbatim); **Medium** on the
lead time, which is inferred from how Posts meet.

## 6. The eligibility fact that drives the pitch — and VFW's own membership rule on top of it

Retained from v1.0 § 4, which reversed Annex C v1.1 and still stands.

**§ 51.1-142.2 B 3 bars** purchase of service credit "included in the calculation of any
retirement allowance received or to be received from this or another retirement system,
**except as otherwise required by Chapter 1223 of Title 10 of the United States Code**."
Chapter 1223 is **Reserve Component** retirement. Counsel and the Red Team have both
construed this and agree (Annex A § 4; Red Team **S-4**).

| Cohort | Eligibility under B 3 | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Short-service active-duty veterans** — 180+ consecutive days, no military retirement | **Eligible**, subject to the unresolved second limb below | **High** (statute, verified verbatim by Red Team) |
| **Guard and Reserve members and retirees** with 180+ consecutive days of qualifying full-time service | **Eligible, and affirmatively protected** — the only cohort whose eligibility survives a military retirement allowance | **High** |
| **Regular (20-year active-duty) retirees** | **Barred** by the first limb | **High** |

**The open limb, which cuts only against the first cohort.** B 3 also bars purchase "if
there is a balance in a defined contribution account that serves as a primary retirement
account related to such service." Red Team S-4 flags that **Blended Retirement System**
service — mandatory for entrants on or after 2018-01-01 — carries government TSP
contributions attributable to that service. **How VRS administers that limb is unknown**
(items 9–10 of the VRS data request; U-11).

**Now lay VFW's own membership rule over it.** VFW eligibility has three conjunctive
requirements — citizenship, honourable service, and **service in a war, campaign or
expedition on foreign soil or in hostile waters**, proven by an authorised campaign
medal, receipt of Hostile Fire or Imminent Danger Pay, or 30 consecutive / 60
non-consecutive days in Korea (VFW By-Laws and Manual of Procedure § 101).

**The consequence, and it is favourable.** VFW's filter is **expeditionary service**,
which is **orthogonal to retirement status.** So:

- VFW membership **spans all three B 3 cohorts** — the one-enlistment veteran, the
  deployed Guard or Reserve member, and the 20-year retiree. No other candidate
  organisation's membership does. Confidence **High** (the rule is documented and
  retirement-blind).
- Which means **VFW is the only candidate that can make the whole argument** — "this
  reaches veterans across our membership" — rather than a cohort-specific one.
- **But** VFW's filter excludes the **never-deployed** veteran, including the Guard
  member with 180+ days of qualifying full-time service who never served abroad. So
  VFW is a broad proxy for the beneficiary population, **not a complete one**.
  Confidence **High** on the exclusion as a matter of the rule; **Low** on its size, as
  **no data on how many Virginia veteran public employees are non-deployed was
  located** (U-13).

**Flag to the Drafting Specialist and the Editor, carried forward from v1.0 and now
more pointed.** The position paper's "how many people" paragraph still has to be
rewritten — Red Team S-4 requires it for accuracy. With VFW as sponsor the rewrite is
*easier* than it would have been with a Guard-specific sponsor: the paper can state all
three cohorts honestly, including the barred one, because the sponsor's membership
includes all three.
